and i was happy until the schoolbell rangwhich took the form of

saturday the twentyfirst

my troubles so im going to write you a ream matilda anne whether you

like it or not and i must begin by telling you about the shack itself

and how i got here all the way out from montreal dinkydunk in his

kindly way kept doing his best to key me down and make me not expect

read and whistle home sweet home he kept saying it would be hard

do you love me i demanded hanging on to him right in front of the
